BAILANDO AL RITMO

CHOREOGRAPHY EVENT
THEME: MODERN HIP HOP & POPPING

  • Each team should comprise 8-10 participants only.
  • Each team must combine Bollywood and English songs from the 2010s (2010-2020) and present a performance- having a duration of 5-6 minutes. Failing to keep within the said duration will result in negative marking.
  • Participants are at the liberty of choosing the number of songs.
  • Teams may use props as per their wish.
  • The duration of the song shall be of 8 minutes including stage set up.
  • No vulgarity shall be entertained. The lyries and the costumes should not be offensive in any manner. Negative marking will be provided if these points are not taken into consideration.
  • The teams are required to submit 2copies of their track at the registration desk in mp3 format only. Nothing but pen drives will be accepted.
  • In case of a power failure or technical glitches during the performance, the team will be permitted to restart their performance.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Expression - 10

Co-ordination - 10

Creativity - 5

Costume - 5

Overall Impact - 10

Relevance to the Theme - 10

Total - 50
