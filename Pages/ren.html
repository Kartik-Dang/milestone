RENAISSANCE

MODELLING EVENT

  • The theme for this year is Celestial Elegance.
  • The participants are to create Futuristic and Avant-Garde outfit.
  • Each team must have 2 models.
  • Each team will be given a chance to present 3walks (2 solo walks+ 1duo walk).
  • The performance should only be in English.
  • Participants are expected to adhere to our event's dress code standards. Attire that does not meet these standards may result in disqualification.
  • The participants must refrain from directly replicating outfits. However. we encourage creative interpretations and inspirations.
  • The participants are to arrange for the musie and props they wish to use. The tracks must be submitted in labelled pen drives at the registration desk.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Costume - 10

Originality - 10

Confidence - 10

The Walk - 10

Overall Impact - 10

Total - 50
