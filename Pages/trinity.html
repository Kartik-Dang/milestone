TRINITY

MUSIC, DANCE & DRAMA
THEME: POP CULTURAL

  • Only 1 participant per school is allowed.
  • A total of 3 - 4 mins will be allowed per performance.
  • The participant is required to act, sing and dance (all three) in accordance to the theme given.
  • A minimum of 30 sec of performance in each category (singing, acting and dancing) is mandatory.
  • The participant must bring a pen drive with their track. and the same has to be submitted to the event head at the registration desk.
  • The decision of the judges shall be final and binding.

Expression - 10

Creativity and Content - 10

Vocals - 10

Transition - 10

Overall Impact - 10

Total - 50
