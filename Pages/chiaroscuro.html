CHIAROSCURO

SKETCHING & SHADING EVENT
THEME: STILL LIFE

  • Each participating school can be represented by 1 participants only.
  • The sketches can be prepared using charcoal and graphite. The use of any sorts of colour is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants are requested to bring their own art material for the event. Only A3 sheet will be provided by the host school.

  • One common object will be provided to each school to sketch. The participants will be given a mystery box later on in the event.
