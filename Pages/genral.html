GENERAL RULES

  • The registration form should be filled by 15th of August. The form is to be filled online and is available along with the mail.
  • Change of participants will not be permitted once the forms are filled. The participants are to note that the names filled in the forms must be spelled correctly. No changes will be permitted later.
  • Each participating school will be charged a nominal registration fee of Rs. 750/- (to be paid in cash) per participant.
  • A maximum of 2teacher escorts can accompany the team.
  • Lunch will be provided by the host school.
  • Participants must maintain anonymity, ensuring no indication of their school's identity. School uniforms are not permitted.
  • Students of classes IX-XII only can take part in the events.
  • A participant can take part in only one event.
  • Kindly send the meal preference (VEG/NON VEG) as a total number for the meals.
  • The participants are required to submit labelled pen drives/SD cards at the registration desk for formatting.
